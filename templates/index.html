Core Capabilities

Built for Modern Smart Municipalities

CleanTrack AI seamlessly blends edge artificial intelligence, spatial clustering algorithms, and an intuitive dispatch dashboard to keep urban neighborhoods pristine.

AI Waste Detection

Citizens upload a photo and our vision inference models automatically classify waste into Plastics, Organic, Paper, or Hazardous debris with transparent confidence metrics.

Smart Hotspot Mapping

Using Scikit-Learn DBSCAN spatial clustering, CleanTrack AI aggregates nearby complaints into dense hotspot zones, pinpointing chronic municipal overflow areas.

Real-Time Tracking

Citizens receive transparent updates across a 5-stage progress timeline, showing review status, team dispatch, and final verification proof.

Cleanup Management

Administrators triage complaints using explainable algorithmic priority scoring, assign designated sanitation teams, and verify after-cleanup photo evidence.

End-to-End Workflow

How CleanTrack AI Works

From citizen observation to verified municipal resolution in 5 transparent steps.

1

Report Waste

Citizen takes a photo of street trash and confirms GPS coordinates on an interactive map.

2

AI Analysis

Vision model categorizes the material and computes initial hazard urgency score.

3

Location Mapping

Report is mapped and clustered into ward-level density hotspots via DBSCAN.

4

Cleanup Assignment

Admin dispatches nearest active sanitation squad with prioritized route.

5

Resolution

Sanitation crew uploads after-cleanup proof photo and citizen is notified.

Our Mission

Empowering Citizens and Municipalities for Zero-Waste Cities

CleanTrack AI was engineered to eliminate informal dump yards and accelerate municipal responsiveness. By democratizing waste reporting with automated vision AI and data-driven prioritization, we ensure municipal resources are dispatched where they are needed most urgently.

  • Transparent Accountability:

    Citizens can inspect full status audit trails and after-cleanup photo evidence.

  • Explainable AI Prioritization:

    Clear breakdown of priority scores based on hazard type, public proximity, and complaint age.

  • Spatial Clustering:

    Prevent chronic accumulation through automated DBSCAN density monitoring.
